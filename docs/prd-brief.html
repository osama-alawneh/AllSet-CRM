01

Why build it

Generic CRMs don't understand window cleaning: they can't price by pane count, stories, or screens, don't map a neighborhood, don't let crews claim jobs, and don't model repeat service. Today it's spreadsheets and a buggy hand-built tool. ClearView fixes exactly that — one place to see the neighborhood, win work, dispatch crews, and get paid, with the money numbers visible only to the owner.

03

What it does

◎

Live neighborhood map

Click a house → drop a colored pin (won / lost / follow-up / new) → it creates a lead. Click any pin to open its lead. Pin ⇄ lead, two-way linked.

◆

Leads pipeline

Drag cards across New → Follow-up → Won → Lost. Dropping into Won automatically creates a job.

✦

Jobs your crew claims

Cleaners claim a job and it locks to them — nobody else can take it. Track Unclaimed → Claimed → In progress → Done.

☺

Customers, connected

Every customer's full history — jobs, invoices, leads — in one profile. Search a name and see a card with phone + address to pick the right person.

$

Invoices & PDF

Build an invoice from a job, print it to PDF, mark it paid. Revenue rolls up on the dashboard — owner only.

▦

Dashboard & export

Revenue, jobs, win rate, overdue invoices, a live map snapshot. Export any list to Excel.

How the pieces connect

Customer ──1───*── Lead ──(won)──▶ Job ──(done)──▶ Invoice
   │                 │                │               │
   │                 └── shown as a   │               └── line items, PDF,
   │                     map pin      │                   status: draft/sent/paid
   ├──1───*── Job         (color =    └── claimed by ONE cleaner (locks)
   └──1───*── Invoice      status)
One customer holds many leads, jobs, and invoices. A lead becomes a job when won; a finished job becomes an invoice. Nothing is a dead end — every record links back to the customer and forward to its next step.
05

On the phone

📲

Now — installable app (PWA) Phase 1

One tap adds ClearView to the home screen with its own icon, full-screen, works on any phone. No app store, no fees. Reps and cleaners are in the field on day one.

📸

Later — native app Phase 2

A real iOS/Android app for crews: before/after photos, GPS routing, solid offline. Shares the same code, added when the field features earn the store fees.

08

What "done" looks like (MVP)

A rep drops a pin, creating a lead on the map and in the pipeline. Dragging it to Won spins up a job; a cleaner claims it and it locks to them. The owner builds an invoice from that job, prints a PDF, marks it paid — and watches revenue update on a dashboard that reps and cleaners can't see the money on. Every list exports to Excel. The whole thing installs on a phone.
